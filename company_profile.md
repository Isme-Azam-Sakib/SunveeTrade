## About Us
Sunvee Trade International was established in the year 2014 as a high-class machine-oriented trims and accessories unit within the Sunvee Trade International. In 2014, Sunvee Trade International expanded its accessories hub and delivers today complete packaging, apparel branding, and supply chain solutions. The company aspires to be the one-stop accessory hub partnering with global brands and manufacturers, supporting brand identity by adding value into the supply chain process.
 
**Management & Organization:**
The overall management of the company is running with its Management Board. The Board decides and formulates policies and provides guidelines to the managers, officers, and executives for smooth operation. The Managing Director looks after overall management. Qualified and experienced technical, administrative, marketing, accounting, and commercial professionals work in the organization.

## Strategic Business Units & Products
 
Sunvee provides a wide variety of trims and accessories directly attached to garments to help create and strengthen brand identity.
 
### 1. Narrow Fabrics & Elastics
*   **Jacquard & Printed Woven Elastic (Micro Polyester yarn):** Soft and breathable. Microfibers are finer than silk, cotton, and wool, providing breathability, wind, and water resistance.
*   **Printed Woven Elastic & Neck Tape (Cationic Polyester Yarn):** Anti-resistant, highly elastic, and features strong color stability.
*   **Crochet, Jacquard, Printed Woven, Draw String (Recycle Polyester):** Quick-drying, high-performance, and sustainable (reduces landfill and ocean pollution).
*   **Jacquard & Printed Woven Elastic (Recycle Nylon):** Circular option that significantly reduces CO2 emissions and saves manufacturing energy.
*   **Drawstring (Vortex Cotton Yarn):** Fast output, strong spinnability, simple operation.
*   **Crochet Elastic (Monofilament Nylon Yarn):** UV resistant, excellent tensile strength, unaffected by wet/dry conditions.
*   **Draw String & Neck Tape (Organic Cotton):** Eco-friendly, worker-safe, empowers farmers.
*   **Printed Woven Elastic & Neck Tape (Coolmax Polyester):** Keeps the wearer cool and dry, 100% recycled resources.
